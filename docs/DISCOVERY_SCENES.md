# Discovery illustrations

The homepage's four-pillars card now uses four jade monuments with perspective faces, travelling light edges, softly pulsing crowns and a shared flowing foundation. Without a chart, the Chinese labels mean year/month/day/hour and the lower slots remain empty. A calculated chart supplies the actual stems, branches and their element colours. Unknown birth time retains the question mark.

The selected-day card uses a shaded central sphere, a clock ring, twelve sample nodes and a golden marker completing an illustrative orbit in 24 seconds. It is a diagram of the selected date, not a live clock or sunrise/sunset model. Calculated branches use their branch element colour. The existing civil two-hour sampling and mean-solar calculation remain unchanged. The five-elements card is unchanged.

Both SVG scenes respect the shared pause, background pause, hidden-page state and reduced-motion rules. No animation interval or extra calculation was added. Verified with TypeScript, the Pages build/asset check, desktop and 320×640 visual inspection, and computed pause states for the orbit and pillar lights.
